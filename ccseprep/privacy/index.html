CCSE Prep — Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · ANAVASIS LLC (“we”).

Summary

CCSE Prep has no account and no server of ours. Your progress, settings and purchases stay on your device.

Stored on your device

Answers, mistakes, bookmarks, exam history, language, exam date and reminder settings. Deleting the app deletes them. “Reset progress” in the app clears them too.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Ads (Android free version only)

The free Android version shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy settings. AdMob may use the advertising ID and device information as described in Google’s policy. Pro removes ads. The iOS app has no ads and no tracking.

AI tutor (when available)

If the AI tutor is enabled, the messages you type to it (and the question you ask about) are sent to Google’s Gemini models through Firebase to produce an answer. They are not linked to your identity by us and are not used for tracking. Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from the genuine app. Do not type personal information into the tutor.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the CCSE test and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
