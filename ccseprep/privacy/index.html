CCSE Prep — Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Applies to the iPhone app and the Android app (version 1.0.0 and later) and this page.

CCSE Prep: Nacionalidad 2026 (“CCSE Prep”, “the app”) is made by ANAVASIS LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no email collection. Your study progress stays on your device.
  • The iPhone app shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK and never asks to track you. The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob; CCSE Prep Pro removes them.
  • When you use the AI tutor, the text needed to answer you is sent to Google’s Gemini models through Firebase.
  • We never sell your personal information and we don’t run any server that receives information from the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Your answers, mistakes, bookmarks, mock-exam history, readiness, language, exam date and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them, and “Reset progress” in the app clears them. If you have turned on your device’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android/Google backup), this data may be included in those backups, which are controlled by Apple or Google and you — never by us.

2. Advertising (free Android version only)

iPhone: the iPhone app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask for tracking permission, and no ad-related data is collected or shared.

Android: the free Android version shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: banner ads on some screens and, at natural break points (for example after a practice session or a mock exam), an occasional full-screen ad. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s ads SDK may collect and use your device’s advertising ID (subject to your device settings), approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google’s use of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/EEA and the UK), the free Android version shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only after that step. You can consent, refuse or manage the individual purposes, and change your choice at any time in the app’s Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline personalisation you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

With CCSE Prep Pro, the app requests and shows no ads.

3. CCSE Prep Pro (optional purchase)

CCSE Prep Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks all 320 practice questions, unlimited mock exams, the full results breakdown and mistakes review, and the AI tutor. It is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) on iPhone or Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see your name, payment card or billing details; the app only receives confirmation from Apple or Google that Pro is unlocked, so it can enable the features and restore your purchase. On Android, Pro also removes all ads.

4. The AI tutor (Google Gemini via Firebase)

When you use the AI tutor, the app sends the message you type, the question you are asking about (with its answer and explanation) and your chosen language to Google’s Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic, a Google service, to produce a reply. No name, email or account identifier is sent, because the app has none; as with any internet request, Google receives your device’s IP address. Firebase App Check confirms that requests come from the genuine app, using Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity; this does not identify you. Google processes this data under the Firebase and Gemini terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Please don’t type personal information into it. The AI tutor is part of Pro and may not be enabled in every version.

5. App settings (Firebase Remote Config)

When the app starts it may download its feature settings (for example whether an optional feature is switched on) from Google’s Firebase Remote Config. To do this, Firebase creates a random installation ID for the app on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID, and contains none of your study data. Google processes it under the Firebase terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Reminders

The optional daily study reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, with your permission. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

7. Ratings

After some use the app may ask Apple’s or Google’s in-app review prompt to appear. Ratings and reviews are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we receive only the public reviews you choose to post.

8. What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection.
  • No selling of personal information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • No analytics SDKs and no data brokers. The third-party services the app uses are: Google AdMob and Google’s consent form in the free Android version only; Google’s Firebase AI Logic and App Check (the AI tutor); Google’s Firebase Remote Config (app settings); Apple’s and Google’s own purchase and review frameworks.
  • No servers of ours that receive information from the app.

10. Children

CCSE Prep is a study tool for adults preparing for Spain's CCSE nationality test. It is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: change your consent in Settings → Ad privacy options, or remove ads with Pro.
  • AI: the AI tutor is optional; don’t use it if you prefer not to send text to Google.
  • Delete everything: delete the app — your study data lives only on your device.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA, the UK or certain U.S. states), you may have rights to access, delete or correct personal information, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information about you on our side, most requests are best handled through the controls above and through Apple’s and Google’s own tools; you can also contact us and we will help.

12. Security and retention

All of the app’s network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Data received by Google or Apple is retained according to their policies.

13. This page

This page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard server logs (such as IP address) to deliver and secure the site.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
